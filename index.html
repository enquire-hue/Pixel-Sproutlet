A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get growing.

What does a website cost?

Every project receives a tailored quote based on pages, features, content and delivery requirements. Supplier costs and any ongoing services are itemised so you can see the full commitment.

Can I bring an existing website?

Yes. A refresh begins with a review of your current platform, content and access. We’ll scope suitable improvements or discuss a rebuild where needed.

Who provides the content?

We agree this in your project scope. You can supply approved copy and images, or request separately scoped help. Image permissions and business information are confirmed before launch.

How long will it take?

Your schedule depends on scope, content readiness and feedback. We agree milestones after discovery and explain how dependencies affect the launch date.

What happens after launch?

You receive a handover and the agreed access. Optional care can cover scoped maintenance and small content edits; hosting and supplier arrangements are explained in your quote.
